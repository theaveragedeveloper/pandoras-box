# Water Purification Notes (SAMPLE)

Boiling: rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft). Kills bacteria, viruses, protozoa.

Filtration: 0.2 micron filter removes bacteria and protozoa. Does not remove viruses — follow with chemical treatment.

Chemical: unscented household bleach, 2 drops per litre, wait 30 minutes. Double the dose for cloudy water.

Local sources: Ponkapoag Pond, Houghton's Pond, Neponset River. All surface water must be treated before drinking.
